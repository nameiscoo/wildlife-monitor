How it works: MobileNet turns each frame into a feature fingerprint and a k-nearest-neighbours classifier trained on the labelled photos names the species (transfer learning; every photo is also learned mirrored). Training photos come from iNaturalist under Creative Commons licences. Everything is stored in this browser's IndexedDB. The wound check is a colour heuristic. The camera needs https, localhost, or plain Chrome.
